Season 12

The Finals Season 12 Patch Notes

Patch 12.0 PRIME TIME — destruction overhaul, the Infiltrator and a full balance pass, summarised for ranked players.

Updated October 21, 2026 6 min read
Season 12Patch 12.0PRIME TIMEBalance changes

Patch 12.0 launched October 20, 2026 and kicks off Season 12 — PRIME TIME. It's a huge update: a rebuilt destruction system, reworked Arenas, the Infiltrator toolkit, Solo Showdown, a new Sponsor system and a sweeping balance pass. Here's a condensed rundown of everything that matters for ranked play. See our Season 12 tier list for how it all shakes out.

New content

Destruction overhaul

Gradual Destruction lets buildings hinge, sag and bend; Suspended Structures now swing; Blast Waves from Breach Charge, Breach Drill, Thermal Bore, C4, RPG-7, Frag Grenade, CL-40, MGL-32, barrels and more push debris and shove the environment around; debris piles build up as buildings fall. Smoke and toxic gas are now dynamic, conform to the environment and can be dispersed by explosions.

The Infiltrator (Light)

New Specialisation True Sight and gadgets Mimic Device and Translocator bring camouflage, counter-play and swap-escapes to Light.

Arenas, modes & systems

Monaco, Seoul, Skyway Stadium and Las Vegas Stadium get layout reworks and visual upgrades under a new game-wide lighting system. Solo Showdown (beta) is a 12-player, three-round solo tournament with eight mini-modes. Sponsors are now a questing system earning Fans for World Tour rank; VRs are removed; Dynamic Commentary, a rebuilt HUD and menus, and a footsteps rework are all in.

Balance changes

The Light and Heavy health changes make the Respec Order test permanent, so many weapon damage values were retuned to match.
